Peering

In general I maintain an open peering policy and peer at physical IXPs. Check out PeeringDB for detailed and up-to-date information.

You will need to:

  • Have a public ASN and a matching PeeringDB entry.
  • Have a BGP-capable router connected to the public VLAN of a physical IXP.
  • Implement reasonable security practices when it comes to routes. MANRS is preferred, RPKI is the absolute minimum.
  • Be reachable within a reasonable timeframe in case of issues. Please at least provide a dedicated NOC email.

If that sounds good to you, please send an email to peering@as200242.net.
